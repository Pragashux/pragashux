## Full description

AI LearnOS is a learning app where an AI layer helps you study—not just a chatbot bolted onto a catalog.

- **Learn with a tutor:** Ask for a simpler explanation, an example, or a quiz on the lesson you are in. AI can be wrong; verify important information.
- **Follow a path:** Courses are organized into modules and lessons with progress, notes, and bookmarks.
- **Practice:** Quizzes and written assignments with feedback.
- **See your progress:** Home recommendations, streaks, and certificates when you complete requirements.
- **Subscribe (when enabled):** Pro and Premium tiers unlock more AI help and certificates. On Android, digital subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing.

AI LearnOS is built for adult learners and career changers. It is not a childcare or K–12 classroom product.
